Cookie Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

1. Summary

speedspin.com does not set any cookies.

We do not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, tracking pixels, web beacons, local storage for tracking, device fingerprinting, or any similar technology.

Because we set no cookies and use no similar technologies requiring consent, the Site displays no cookie consent banner. This is not an oversight — there is nothing to consent to.

2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website you visit. They are widely used to make websites work, to remember preferences, and to report on how a site is used. Similar technologies — local storage, session storage, pixels and beacons — can serve the same purposes.

Under the EU ePrivacy rules and the GDPR, storing information on your device, or accessing information already stored on it, generally requires your consent unless it is strictly necessary to deliver a service you have requested.

3. Why this Site has none

speedspin.com is a static, business-to-business marketing website. It has no user accounts, no login, no shopping basket, no contact form, and no analytics. There is no session to maintain and no behaviour we measure, so no cookie is necessary and none is set.

4. Third-party requests

The Site makes no third-party requests at all. Every asset — including the typefaces — is served from our own domain. There is no embedded video, no social media widget, no chat widget, no advertising network, no tag manager, and no remotely loaded font.

This means that loading speedspin.com discloses your IP address to nobody except us and our hosting provider. No other company learns that you visited.

5. Your hosting provider

The Site is served by Vercel Inc., which processes standard server request data — including IP address — to deliver the Site and protect it against abuse. This is a server-side log rather than a cookie, and nothing is stored on your device.

6. Managing cookies generally

Although this Site sets none, you can control cookies from any website through your browser settings. Most browsers let you view what is stored, block cookies from particular sites or all sites, and delete cookies already saved. Guidance is available in the help documentation for Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge.

Blocking cookies will not affect your use of speedspin.com.

7. Changes

If we later introduce cookies or similar technologies — for example if we add analytics or a contact form — we will update this policy before doing so, and will implement a consent mechanism where the law requires one.
