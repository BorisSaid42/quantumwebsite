4. What we collect and why

a. Information you send us by email

The Site lists contact@speedspin.com and uses mailto: links. If you email us, we receive whatever you choose to include — typically your name, business email address, company, and the content of your enquiry.

  • Purpose: to respond to your enquiry, evaluate a potential commercial relationship, and keep a record of our business correspondence.
  • Lawful basis: our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in responding to business enquiries and pursuing commercial relationships. Where we are negotiating or performing a contract with you, Article 6(1)(b) may also apply.

b. Technical data from serving the Site

Our hosting provider processes standard technical information required to deliver the Site, which may include your IP address, browser type and user agent, the pages requested, and the date and time of the request. This is used to serve the Site and to maintain its security and integrity.

  • Lawful basis: our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in operating and securing our website.

We do not use this technical data to build profiles or to identify individual visitors, and we do not combine it with other data.

We do not collect anything else. The Site has no other source of personal data.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not share personal data for advertising or marketing purposes.
  • We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children. The Site is a business-to-business resource and is not directed at children or at consumers.

You may request further information about these safeguards using the contact details above.
